Overview

My work at Google was mostly behind the scenes. I built the demos and prototypes that showed enterprise clients what was actually possible with Google's AI tools, before any of it turned into a signed contract or a real product. Over two years, that meant working across many different industries, each with its own goals, stakeholders, and idea of what "AI powered" actually needed to look like.

The work below is grouped by client and industry. Specific deliverables remain under NDA, but the problems, approaches, and outcomes are described as I'd walk through them in a technical interview.

Dexcom

Healthcare · Medical Device

Dexcom needed a customer support transformation paired with AI powered agent enablement, a high stakes initiative given the medical device context, where a support interaction can directly affect someone managing a health condition. I managed the discovery phases, facilitated alignment syncs across a large cross functional group of technical and business stakeholders, and orchestrated complex, large scale solution demos on an abbreviated timeline.

Pfizer

Life Sciences

On the Google engagement, I designed an AI assisted workflow on Google Agentspace that helps a pharmaceutical company triage and respond to regulatory queries from the FDA, turning a high stakes flood of incoming questions into a clear, prioritized dashboard with routing and human approvals built in.

The FDA sends Pfizer complex drug related questionnaires by email, and the challenge was that these are high priority, high volume, and easy to lose in an inbox. I designed an Agentspace experience where the AI ingests each incoming FDA email, extracts it, and surfaces it to the right point of contact as a flagged, high priority notification right on their dashboard, so the most important items are visible immediately instead of buried. From there, the user opens the query and the AI classifies the FDA questions by category and routes each one to the right clinician, with a manager reviewing and approving the assignments, human in the loop at every step. Each clinician then works their portion inside Agentspace, using connectors to pull in the information they need, compiles their answer, and sends it back to the manager for final approval. My job was taking all of that dense, regulated, multi source data and shaping it into a UI that's easy to scan, look up, edit, and act on.

PayPal, Global Payments, Amex

Fintech · Payments

Across PayPal, Global Payments, and Amex, the throughline was making the post purchase experience less painful, specifically, how an AI assistant could help someone who bought something through one of these payment methods and needed to return it or raise a claim. The work leaned on generative AI, multimodality, and AI orchestration to make that journey feel seamless instead of like a support ticket.

Global Payments, four use cases

  • Product ways of working, when an item a customer wants is out of stock, the AI asks for human permission before automatically adding it back to the cart once it's available again.
  • Small to Medium Business in a Box, how small and medium businesses could adopt the AI platform to support their own operations.
  • Developer experience, both internal and external, improving how developers work with the platform.
  • Merchant onboarding, streamlining how new merchants get set up.

AIG, Brown & Brown, Liberty Mutual

Insurance

For this cluster of insurance clients, the core problem was showcasing an underwriting workflow through a multi agentic system, delivered via a custom UI portal. I designed how multiple AI agents could work together, each one handling a distinct part of the claims process, connected through custom orchestration, rather than one monolithic assistant trying to do everything.

Alongside the agent orchestration, I designed claims management dashboards giving managers a clear view of every claim: which broker it's assigned to, its current progress, and full case details, with AI assisting the handoff between people so information didn't get lost in the transition.

Mondelez, PepsiCo, Dr Pepper, Mars, Colgate

CPG · Consumer Goods

This group of consumer goods clients wanted to see how Google's Agent Space platform could apply to their own operations, across two distinct use cases:

  • HR self service, employees could ask an AI directly about pay, leave, or other HR questions, getting answers immediately unless the situation genuinely needed a human.
  • Multimodal shelf scanning, pointing a phone camera at a shelf or pantry to see current stock levels and know what needed restocking, turning a manual inventory check into a visual, instant answer.

Papa John's

Restaurant · Retail

I owned the end to end UX for an AI driven solution built to increase customer loyalty and retention through a hyper personalized loyalty program, and to drive revenue growth through dynamic pricing and targeted promotions, all powered by AI and its multimodal capabilities. The work went from concept through a client ready prototype, and helped secure an $X million contract with Google.

Modivecare

Healthcare · Mobility

Modivecare needed a way to make non emergency medical transportation feel personal at scale. I designed how AI could automatically schedule doctor's appointments and book rides for elderly patients, accounting for each person's language preference and preferred vehicle type based on their specific medical plan, so the experience felt tailored rather than generic, even though it was running for a large population of patients.

Travel Concierge

Internal Google Product

Travel Concierge was an in house Google app built to be a one stop AI travel assistant, covering bookings, itinerary generation, and pre and post trip recommendations. I led the concept from an open ended brief with two other designers, building the flow around AI generated mood boards that shaped destination inspiration, an editable itinerary that always kept the user in control, and contextual recommendations like currency exchange tips, packing suggestions, and a passport expiry check using OCR.

I worked closely with an offshore backend team, subject matter experts, and a technical lead, keeping a shared tracker so every task had a clear owner and status visible to the whole group.

Working this way

None of this work exists as a public demo I can point to. Most of it lives on a Google laptop, under an NDA that won't lift. But the pattern across every one of these clients was the same: take an ambiguous, often unstructured problem, and turn it into something a real team could actually run, with AI doing the parts that scale, and a human always able to step in where it mattered.
