UX Case Study · Collaboration

ASU Chatbot

A chatbot that helps ASU students find bus routes, buildings, and classrooms — in seconds, like a virtual campus guide.

01 · Introduction

Introduction

To design an application that uses the AI chat feature to interact with students in an effective manner.

The idea is to provide bite-size information and help students with their bus routes and directions — further, it could be your virtual campus pal.

Research

We ran research in three phases — preliminary, primary, and secondary — using surveys, pre- and post-test questionnaires, and direct user testing to understand what students actually needed.

Read the full research insights →

Design

We built the prototype in Figma, redesigning the map to match the familiar patterns of Google Maps, and stayed close to ASU's branding throughout.

View the interactive Figma prototype →

Conclusion

We shipped an alpha prototype, with class schedules, admissions, and notifications planned as next steps.

About the team

A team of ASU graduate UX students, focused on helping peers navigate campus and improving the ASU app's overall experience.
