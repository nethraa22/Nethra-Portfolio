UI/UX · Wireframing · Prototyping · Academic Project

Ready To Cook App

A snack box subscription service, launched with a strategic focus on customer preferences identified through market research.

Introduction

An academic project revamping a Snack Box Subscription concept — using feedback from a design manager and real customer preferences, following a standard industry design process.

Brainstorming

Working remotely in Mural over Zoom, our team ran three structured ideation rounds — moderated by our professor — to land on the strongest features for the platform.

"You have been identified as a stakeholder and they want to have you participate in a feature ideation session."

Project brief

After brainstorming, the team built wireframes for three features:

  • Snack Box Subscription — 36 snacks across categories, with size, frequency, and nutrition info at a glance.
  • Custom Snack Box — a themed gift box with a personal message.
  • Virtual Gift Cards — $25, $50, or $100, redeemable at checkout.

Each one was built for a smooth path from selection to purchase.
