Website Re-design

Toronto Cupcake Redesign

A cluttered checkout was costing Toronto Cupcake real customers. I redesigned the site to make ordering effortless.

Contribution
Research, UX Design, UI Design
Tools
Figma, Google Slides, Google Forms
Year
2023

Project overview

Toronto Cupcake sells gourmet, personalized cupcakes — but its outdated site made ordering confusing, and customers didn't trust it enough to check out.

Finding the problem

I tested the site with five users and found three recurring problems:

1

The navigation menu was placed at the bottom of the website.

2

Users had to manually type the quantity of cupcakes.

3

Users were confused while entering delivery location and order details.

How can we improve the UI/UX of the current website to make it accessible to the user?

Solution

Weak layout, poor contrast, and a confusing checkout were hurting the experience end to end. I rebuilt the flow around clarity — simpler navigation, an intuitive ordering process, and a design that felt trustworthy from the first click.
